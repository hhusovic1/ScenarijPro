INT. STARSHIP BRIDGE - NIGHT

The vast, grey SILENCE of the moon. Dust motes hang impossibly still in the weak light. CAPTAIN KIRA (40s, sharp, tired) walks toward a ruined DOME, her footsteps crunching.

Behind her, the wreckage of a spacecraft smolders. She doesn't look back.

She reaches the dome entrance. Pauses. The door is already open.
